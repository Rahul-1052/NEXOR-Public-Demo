# NEXOR – Healthcare Market Access Analytics Platform

## Live Demo

https://nexor-public-demo.onrender.com

> Note: The application is hosted on Render's free tier. Initial load may take 30–60 seconds due to cold starts.

---

## Overview

NEXOR is a healthcare analytics platform designed to support payer strategy, market access planning, and healthcare decision-making through integrated claims analytics, disease trend monitoring, forecasting, and opportunity identification.

The platform combines insurance performance insights with pharmaceutical demand forecasting to help stakeholders identify revenue opportunities, monitor disease burden, and prioritize healthcare strategies.

---

## Key Features

### Insurance Dashboard

* Total Claims Analysis
* Coverage Percentage Monitoring
* Revenue Leakage Detection
* Denied Claims Tracking
* Payer Performance Analysis
* Claims Trend Visualization

### Pharma Dashboard

* Disease Burden Analysis
* Opportunity Matrix
* Monthly Disease Demand Trends
* Disease Demand Forecasting
* Market Opportunity Prioritization

### AI Assistant

* NVIDIA NIM Powered
* Llama 3.1 Integration
* Platform-Specific Healthcare Analytics Guidance
* FAQ + LLM Hybrid Response System
