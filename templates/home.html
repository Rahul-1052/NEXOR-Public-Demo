Welcome

This platform was designed to unify claims, payer, and disease data into a single decision-support environment. It enables stakeholders to evaluate expenditure patterns, identify leakage exposure, monitor disease demand, and prioritize future opportunities.

The name NEXOR reflects its role as a central hub connecting multiple data sources and transforming raw healthcare data into actionable insights.

NEXOR AI

NEXOR AI is the conversational intelligence layer of the platform, designed to help users interpret dashboard metrics, trends, and strategic signals more efficiently.

It connects user queries with relevant project knowledge across claims, payer performance, disease trends, opportunity analysis, and forecast insights.

The assistant follows a hybrid approach — it first checks structured knowledge using similarity matching, and when required, generates contextual responses using a local language model.

What it helps explain

  • Payer performance and leakage exposure
  • Claim trends and forecast interpretation
  • Disease demand and opportunity signals
  • Dashboard metrics and strategic insights
